        Brent and WTI prices are Capital.com's live spot quotes, refreshed about every 15 seconds; if Capital.com is unavailable the page falls back to Yahoo Finance futures, which are delayed by around ten minutes and are labelled as such. The dollar index, refined products, the curve and OVX still come from Yahoo and are delayed. Inventory data updates on Wednesdays and positioning on Fridays, so they inform direction rather than timing. Intraday suggestions follow your trading window of 08:00 to 23:59 Dubai: no new intraday trades outside it, and every intraday position closed by 23:59, before overnight funding is charged at 02:00. The suggestion is produced by a transparent rule set and is decision support, not financial advice.
